About DT-Agent

DecodingTrust Agent — a real-world simulation platform for advanced red-teaming of AI agents.

Our Mission

DT-Agent is a real-world simulation platform for advanced red-teaming of AI agents. Our mission is to provide researchers and practitioners with the tools needed to rigorously evaluate AI agent security across diverse, real-world domains.

The platform encompasses 30+ high-fidelity sandbox environments (e.g., Gmail, PayPal, Databricks) spanning over 15 domains, with over 500 benign and malicious tasks per domain. All evaluated risks are derived directly from domain-specific safety and security policies such as FINRA in Finance and the Salesforce AI Use Policy.
